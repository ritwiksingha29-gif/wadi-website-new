Our Story

Wadi Al Naam Technical Services LLC was founded with a simple yet powerful goal: to bring professional, high-quality technical services to homes and businesses across Dubai. Over the years, we have grown from a small team of dedicated craftsmen to a comprehensive service provider specializing in plaster work, carpentry, and wooden work.

Our team comprises skilled professionals with years of experience in their respective fields. We take pride in our attention to detail, commitment to deadlines, and ability to handle projects of all sizes – from small residential repairs to large commercial installations.
